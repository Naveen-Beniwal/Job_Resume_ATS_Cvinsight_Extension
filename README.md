# 🚀 CVInsight: Your AI Job Search Copilot

Welcome to the **CVInsight Extension**, your ultimate companion for dominating the job hunt. 

Why pay for expensive LinkedIn Premium features when you can get them for free? CVInsight sits right in your browser and automatically analyzes jobs on LinkedIn, Naukri, and Internshala as you browse, giving you an unfair advantage in your job search.

## ✨ Supercharge Your Job Search
With CVInsight, you get premium-level insights without leaving the job board:

- 🎯 **1-Click Profile Matching:** Instantly see a Match Score (0-100%) telling you exactly how well your skills align with the job description.
- 📄 **1-Click Resume Tailoring:** Automatically generate a custom, ATS-friendly resume perfectly tailored to the specific job you are looking at.
- 📌 **1-Click Job Tracking:** Save jobs directly to your CVInsight Job Tracker dashboard so you never lose track of what you've applied for.
- 💡 **Skill Gap Analysis:** Discover exactly which keywords and skills you are missing before you hit "Apply".

## 🛠️ How to Install (Takes 30 seconds!)

Since we are currently awaiting official approval on the Chrome Web Store and Microsoft Edge Add-ons, you can install the beta version directly by following these simple steps:

1. **Download the Extension:** Download the `CVInsight_Extension_Production.zip` file from this repository.
2. **Extract the ZIP:** Unzip/Extract the downloaded file into a folder on your computer.
3. **Open Extensions Page:** Open Google Chrome (or Edge) and type `chrome://extensions/` in your URL bar and hit Enter.
4. **Enable Developer Mode:** Turn on the **"Developer mode"** toggle in the top right corner of the page.
5. **Load the Extension:** Click the **"Load unpacked"** button in the top left and select the folder where you extracted the ZIP file.

🎉 **Boom! You're done.** Pin the CVInsight logo to your browser toolbar, log in, and open a job on LinkedIn to see the magic happen!

*(Note: We have also included a step-by-step PDF in this repository with screenshots if you need visual help!)*
